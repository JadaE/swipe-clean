Delete them yourself in Google Photos

  1. Tap each “Open in Google Photos” link. It searches your library for that file name.
  2. Check it's the right photo, then tap 🗑 Delete (Move to trash) in Google Photos.
  3. When you're done, open Google Photos → Collections → Trash → Empty trash. Space is only freed after that (otherwise Google empties it after 60 days).

This app can't delete anything. It doesn't have permission to, by design.

Common names like IMG_0001.jpg can repeat in your library, so compare the thumbnail before deleting. Tick ✓ as you go; ticks are saved on this device.

🔒 What this app can and can't do

It only sees the photos you choose in Google's own photo picker, and only to look at them (read-only). It has no permission to delete, edit, move, or share anything, and it can't see the rest of your library.

Nothing leaves your browser. There's no server or account. Your keep/delete choices are saved only on this device, and no photos are stored. Google may forget the photos you picked after a while, but you can always pick again.

You do the deleting. The app gives you a list with a link to each photo. You delete them yourself in Google Photos.
